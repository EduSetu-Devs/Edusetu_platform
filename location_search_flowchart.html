System Flowchart

Location-Based Tutor Search

Flow of Actions (EduSetu Platform)

Step 1 • Start

Student Enters Search Criteria

City, Area / Locality, Subject & Mode (Online / Offline)

↓
Step 2 • Decision

Is Teaching Mode "Offline"?

Check if student wants Home Tuition / In-Person Class

YES (Offline)

Filter tutors strictly living in that same City & Area

NO (Online)

Include verified tutors available across all locations

↓
Step 3 • Academic Match

Match Subject & Grade Level

Select tutors who teach the requested subject and class syllabus

↓
Step 4 • Schedule Check

Check Tutor Availability

Ensure tutor has active time slots on student's preferred days

↓
Step 5 • Ranking Algorithm

Rank Matching Tutors

Sort by Student Ratings, Experience, Verification & Budget

↓
Step 6 • Output

Display Best Tutor Profiles to Student

Show verified profiles with location badges, rating & Book button
